# Market Evidence and Hypotheses

## Evidence boundary

This is a qualitative review of publicly documented capabilities, not an exhaustive market study, product test, pricing comparison, or market-size estimate. Sources were consulted on 3 November 2026. A capability not mentioned by a source must not be treated as absent from that product.

| Solution | Publicly documented capability | Relevance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PrestoStats | Football and flag support, live or post-game entry, online/offline operation, and reports [S1] | Live entry and offline operation already exist in the market |
| PrestoStats Football 2.0 | Editing plays and conditionally inserting plays before or after others [S2] | Corrections and interoperability need direct comparison |
| PrestoStats Flag 2.0 | Field, down, scoring, and guided-entry settings [S3] | Configurable competition variants are a credible expectation |
| Hudl Assist | Video-linked statistics; analyst-led service for most sports and AI for selected sports [S4] | Video analysis is an operational capability, not a trivial add-on |
| TurboStats | Football capture, reports, publishing, and video links [S5] | Capture, coaching, and publishing workflows already overlap |
| Paper and spreadsheets | Current alternative reported by the project owner | Familiarity, autonomy, and switching cost are real competition |

## Product hypotheses

The opportunity is not “live statistics” as an invention. The hypothesis is that a focused service can fit selected clubs and statistics providers better through reliable field operation, fast onboarding, Spanish-first terminology, competition-specific deliverables, and close support.

The following are unvalidated hypotheses, not competitor deficiencies:

- Post-game re-entry consumes meaningful time and introduces avoidable errors.
- Immediate dashboards are valuable to buyers rather than merely attractive to viewers.
- Stadium connectivity makes offline capture a purchase requirement.
- Clear correction consequences and synchronization states improve trust.
- A buyer will pay for a seasonal, tournament, or multi-client service.
- Voice capture produces a net time benefit after review and corrections.
- Mexico is a useful entry market based on the project owner's experience, not proven demand.

## Validation work

Observe at least one real capture workflow and measure preparation, play-entry time, missed actions, corrections, report delay, and synchronization incidents. Interview operators separately from buyers and downstream consumers. Ask for the actual rules, sheets, exports, and approval process rather than validating a generic feature list. Test a concrete commercial offer, preferably with a paid pilot.

Candidate business models—organization/season subscription, tournament fee, and a multi-client provider plan—remain hypotheses. Voice, video, integrations, or customization may become paid options only after measuring their operating costs. No price or buyer commitment is currently validated.
